Gamma tracker

Modeled from options open interest, not real dealer positions. Free sources disagree, so trust the sign of the regime, not the size. Main source: ZeroGEX. Each day's regime is the morning snapshot (about 9:35 New York time, before your entry) when there is one, otherwise the end-of-day reading (NQ levels are converted from NDX options to futures price). GEXBoard and others are second sources. Faded labels (*) mean ZeroGEX had no reading that day and other sources filled in.

Units differ by source: $B/1% is billions per 1% move; QW raw is QuantWheel's own scale. Times are US Eastern.

Positive gamma: dealers are modeled to sell rallies and buy dips (calmer). Negative gamma: they trade with the move (wilder). Gamma flip is where modeled net gamma changes sign; call and put walls are the heaviest modeled strikes. Neither says which way price goes.
